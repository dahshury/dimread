# DimRead icon exploration prompts

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool as `logo-brand` exploration boards.

## Shared direction

Create a DimRead desktop application icon board on a light neutral canvas: one large
full-color rounded-square app icon above two matching monochrome system-tray swatches
(black on white and white on black). Use flat, crisp geometry, a midnight-navy tile,
vivid cobalt/indigo, and a restrained warm-amber accent. The tray mark must be centered
with roughly 20% padding, dense at 16 px, and free of clipping, thin strokes, outlines,
open contours, detached details, and hollow centers. No text, watermark, mockup, or
glossy 3D treatment.

## Concepts

1. **Dusk Window** — a solid rounded window whose descending top edge transitions
   from cool blue to warm amber; the tray companion is the complete filled silhouette.
2. **Quiet Page** — one abstract reading page with a blue face and an amber folded
   corner, reduced to a compact page/bookmark silhouette for the tray.
3. **Dim Dial** — a large brightness dial with a dark coverage wedge and a small warm
   color-temperature segment; the tray mark is a chunky notched disk rather than a ring.
4. **Dimming Drop** — a broad light drop physically fused to a chunky control base,
   using blue above and amber below as one continuous silhouette.
5. **Comfort Canopy** — a single bell-like outer contour that suggests both a reading
   lamp and an open page, with directly joined blue and amber regions.
6. **Filter Layers** — two fused rounded screen/filter panels whose overlap creates an
   amber band and downward dimming motion, reduced to a compact shield-like tray mark.
